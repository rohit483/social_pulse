Why Your Support Matters

Social Pulse started out as a project with a simple goal: to help creators, brands, and everyday users understand their Instagram audience instantly through AI-powered sentiment analysis.

Today, Social Pulse handles Instagram comment scraping, sentiment labeling, and instant feedback processing. While the platform is fully up and running, a few key features and scaling abilities are currently held back by limited solo-developer resources.

Right now, Social Pulse relies on dev-tier AI models, which have usage caps and performance limits. Upgrading to production-grade AI will unlock faster response times, sharper sentiment analysis, better language support, and the ability to serve many more users at once without rate limits.

Here is where your support directly goes:

  • Production AI Services – Sharper sentiment reviews, faster responses, and multi-language support.
  • API & Proxy Infrastructure – Stable scraping capabilities without getting blocked.
  • Hosting & Server Costs – Keeping the platform online, secure, fast, and free to use.

Every bit helps, no matter the amount. Your support goes straight into making Social Pulse smarter, faster, and accessible to everyone.
